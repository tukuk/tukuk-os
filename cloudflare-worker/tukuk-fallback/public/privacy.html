This policy describes what data Tukuk-OS ("the service") collects, why it is collected, and the rights you have over it. The service is a free web search engine.

1. Summary

  • No account or sign-up is required.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use advertising tools to build user profiles.
  • We allow Google AdSense ads for financial support.

2. Data collected

2.1 Server logs

When you use the search engine, our servers record the IP address, the time, and the route requested. These logs are used to detect and block attacks (e.g. rate limiting) and to keep the service stable. Logs are not used for advertising.

2.2 Cookies

The service itself uses no cookies. No search sessions are stored and no trackers are installed.

Google AdSense and ad providers may use cookies to measure ad effectiveness. Those cookies are controlled by Google, not by us. You can manage them via Google Ad Center or your browser privacy settings.

2.3 Indexed content

The service indexes public web pages so that search works. For each page we store the title, description, up to 6000 characters of text, links, and the URL. We respect robots.txt and do not index blocked pages.

2.4 Third parties

Third parties that may receive data:

  • Google AdSense — advertising
  • Cloudflare — content delivery network, TLS certificates, and firewall

3. Processing purposes

Data is processed to provide search functionality, keep the service stable, protect the system from attacks, and monetize through ads.

4. Retention

  • Server logs: kept for at most 30 days.
  • Indexed documents: remain until the source content changes or at the owner's request.

5. User rights

You may request:

  • a copy of the data we store about you;
  • deletion of your log records;
  • removal of your pages from the index — send the URL via the Contact page.

We respond within 14 working days.

6. Third-party copyright

The service indexes third-party pages. The original content remains the copyright of its owners. For removal requests you can contact us and we will drop the URL from the index.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The update date is shown at the top. Significant changes will be noted on the home page.

8. Contact us

Questions about privacy: contact us.
