T Chat

Real chat in the browser — no e-mail, no app.

Features & how it works
Register with one name + password. Passwords are stored as a server-side scrypt hash — we can't read the original. After login you land straight in the 🌍 General room to chat with everyone online. Press ➕ to find other users and open 1-on-1 direct messages (DM).
Images & video: press 📎 (or paste/drag files) to send media — images are compressed in the browser first for speed, video up to 64MB.
Tukuk ID: every account gets a permanent number (example 100042). Friends can find you by that number even if you change your name — DMs and old messages never break.
Settings (⚙️): change name & bio, change password, friend list, find people, block/unblock, and sign out other devices.
📹 Calls: in a DM, press 📹 or 📞 for direct video/voice calls between devices — the incoming screen shows who is calling (name, Tukuk ID, friend or not) before you answer.
🎭 Prank: press the mask button in the send bar to show a fake fullscreen incoming call with a custom name — works even offline. Now with direct dial: enter a real phone number and T Chat opens your phone's dialer with it (call goes out from your own number), plus a 🔊 voice-line soundboard for speakerphone pranks.
📻 Sound Wave: send a text (any length — long ones auto-split into numbered waves) as sound between two devices placed next to each other (ggwave, runs 100% locally) — works even when neither device has internet. 🪪 My card pairs Tukuk IDs by sound: the listener gets one-tap add-friend / DM buttons. Caught texts can be relayed straight into the chat with 📤.
📲 Install: ⚙️ → App to install T Chat on Android/iOS/Windows/Linux/Huawei, or press 🌐 Download (all OS) for the auto-pick page: APK directly on Android/Huawei/ChromeOS, PWA on Windows/Linux/macOS, Add to Home Screen on iOS.
Safety: use a password you don't use elsewhere, and generate recovery codes in ⚙️ Security — one code can reset access if you forget the password.

Powered by ggwave (MIT, runs fully local): text → sound → text. Put two devices next to each other — one plays, one listens — and it works even when neither has internet: no server, no account, no pairing. Turn the volume up. Texts over ~90 characters are split into numbered waves and rejoined automatically with 🧩 on the listening side. 🪪 My card waves your Tukuk ID + name so the other device gets one-tap ➕ Friend and 💬 DM buttons — no typing. 🔊 Audible is reliable; 🤫 Ultrasound is nearly silent but fails more often near noisy speakers.

📦 Download APK (Android · Beta)Want to install without a store? Download T Chat.apk directly — a native wrapper (Android 6.0+) that opens this app fullscreen, including camera/mic calls & attachments. This is a beta version — features are being tested and may change; a stable release follows. Open the file → allow “unknown sources” → Install.
