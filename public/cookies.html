What each choice enables

  • Advertising storage (ad_storage) — lets ad cookies be written.
  • Ad user data (ad_user_data) — sends data to Google for ad targeting.
  • Ad personalisation (ad_personalization) — enables personalised remarketing ads.
  • Analytics storage (analytics_storage) — lets Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity measure how the site is used.

Managing Google ads on the web

Your ad personalisation is also controlled by Google, independently of this site. You can review and opt out at any time through Google Ad Center, and install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Microsoft Clarity data is handled under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Your decision is stored only on this device (in localStorage) — we do not receive it. Read more in the Privacy Policy, or ask a question via Contact.
