How a product gets on this page

Nothing here is paid placement. A tool appears after it has run a real part of Tukuk's own workload for at least a month — a VPS that has hosted the origin, a domain registrar that has moved a nameserver, a keyboard that has survived daily typing. Price matters, but only after reliability: a cheap service that loses data costs more than an expensive one that does not. We list the plan we actually use, the price we actually paid, and we remove an item when we stop using it. If you have had a different experience with something on this page, tell us and we will re-check it.
