Aim down sights for accuracy and a tighter camera. Dash through fire to break line of sight — you are briefly invulnerable. Kills feed a combo multiplier that fades if you stop shooting. Clear a wave and you pick one of three perks — stack damage, fire rate, speed, cooldowns and more into your own build.
