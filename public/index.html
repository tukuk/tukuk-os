Welcome

Tukuk-OS is a free, account-free internet portal that provides web search, AI answers, an app and game store, a 2G chat client, radio, and a creative studio. Everything runs light and high-performance for low-RAM servers (2GB) and works even on weak networks.

Features

  • Web search engine with snippets, filters and facets
  • AI answers and conversation
  • App & game store (one-word apps and games)
  • T Chat 2G messenger with video & voice calls
  • Radio streaming
  • No-code creative studio
  • Works on weak networks including 2G
