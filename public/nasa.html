About the NASA Data Hub on Tukuk-OS

The universe is filled with incredible astronomical phenomena — from solar flares and aurora borealis to distant galaxial collisions billions of light-years away. Tukuk-OS aggregates open data streams provided by space agencies to deliver free, accessible space exploration tools without requiring personal user tracking or API keys.

Key Features & Space Feeds Available

📷 Daily Astrophotography

Every single day, professional astronomers curate a featured photo or video of our cosmos accompanied by detailed scientific commentary written by astrophysicists.

🛰️ ISS Live Telemetry

Track the orbital speed, altitude, and geographical coordinates of the International Space Station in real time as it orbits the Earth at 28,000 km/h.

☄️ Near-Earth Asteroid (NEO) Radar

Monitor real-time trajectories and close-approach distances of cosmic asteroids passing within Earth's orbital neighborhood using NASA NeoWs data.

☀️ Space Weather & Solar Flares

View atmospheric radio flux, solar storm activity, and geomagnetic disturbance alerts measured by space weather observation satellites.

How Astronomy Picture of the Day Works

Since 1995, NASA's APOD program has published a new image daily. Each image is selected by professional astronomers Robert Nemiroff and Jerry Bonnell. The archive contains over 10,000 high-resolution imagery assets covering planetary science, nebulae, star clusters, eclipses, space missions, and historical aerospace photographs.

Educational & Scientific Utility

Whether you are a student learning observational astronomy, a educator preparing physics classroom material, or an avid stargazer, Tukuk-OS makes it effortless to integrate daily space science into your routine. No subscription or registration is required.
