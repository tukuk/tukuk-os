## Edit video without installing anything

Lots of people need a quick video edit — cut the boring part, add captions, put in background music — but do not want to install heavy software or pay a subscription. The **Tukuk video editor** (`tukuk.org/editor-video`) runs right in the browser. Open it, upload your clip, and start editing. No installation, no sign-up, no forced watermark.

## What you can do

- **Trim & merge**: drag the markers, remove unneeded parts, arrange several clips on one timeline.
- **Text & captions**: add a title or caption, change size, color, and position.
- **Effects**: style filters included (per-frame cartoon effects for clips that feel more "alive").
- **Export**: produce a final file you can share to social media.

All processing happens on your side (in the browser), so your file never has to be uploaded anywhere for basic edits — more private, more lightweight.

## For images instead

If all you need is an **image** — crop, resize, remove the background — there is the **Design Studio** (`tukuk.org/design`) and the **Format Converter** (`tukuk.org/converter`) for switching between JPG/PNG and others. One tab is enough for everyday quick work.

## Why use browser tools?

Because they are *wherever you are*. Phone, tablet, or a borrowed computer — the tools stay the same; you only need a browser. And like every Tukuk app, the page can be installed as a **PWA** on your home screen, or (for certain editors) brought to Android as an **APK** from the app store. A good video does not need expensive tools; sometimes an open tab is enough.
