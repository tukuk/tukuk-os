## Edit video without installing anything

Lots of people need a quick video edit — cut the boring part, add captions, put in background music — but do not want to install heavy software or pay a subscription. Modern **browser video editors** run right in the tab: open a page, upload your clip, and start editing. No installation, no sign-up, no forced watermark.

## What to look for in a good editor

- **Trim & merge**: drag the markers, remove unneeded parts, arrange several clips on one timeline.
- **Text & captions**: add a title or caption, change size, color, and position.
- **Effects**: style filters and per-frame looks that make a clip feel more "alive".
- **Export**: produce a final file you can share to social media (usually WebM or MP4).

Prefer editors that do the work **on your side** (in the browser), so your file never has to be uploaded anywhere — more private, more lightweight. Client-side editors use the `<canvas>` and `MediaRecorder` APIs to render and export without a server round-trip.

## For images instead

If all you need is an **image** — crop, resize, or clean up a background — a lightweight browser image tool is enough. Search for one that also runs entirely in the tab, so nothing leaves your device. One tab is all you need for everyday quick work.

## Why use browser tools?

Because they are *wherever you are*. Phone, tablet, or a borrowed computer — the tools stay the same; you only need a browser. Many can be **added to your home screen** as a PWA, feeling like a regular app you can reopen anytime. A good video does not need expensive software; sometimes an open tab is enough.
