## The most annoying problem: cutting out the background

Used to be, removing an image background meant opening Photoshop, tracing manually with the pen tool, or signing up for expensive software. For someone who just needs one product photo on a white background, that is overkill. Modern **background removers** boil it all down: upload an image, the tool detects the subject's edges, and you get a **PNG with a transparent background**.

## How it works

1. Open the page, drag in your picture (portrait, product, sticker).
2. Wait a moment — the tool identifies the subject and masks away the background.
3. Download the result as a transparent PNG, or swap the background to a new color (white for online shops, brand color for posters).

Client-side tools run the segmentation **in the browser**, so your picture does not need to be sent to a remote server for the basic steps — good for privacy, and also for speed on an ordinary connection.

## When to use it

- **Online sellers**: white-background product shots that meet marketplace requirements.
- **Designers & teachers**: cut out subjects for slides, posters, or collages of many photos.
- **Social media content**: avatars and stickers with clean edges.

## A few tips for a clean cut-out

- Shoot or pick a subject with **contrast** against its background — edges come out far cleaner.
- Zoom in and check hair, fur, and thin edges; a small "feather" or "refine edge" pass softens the mask.
- Export as **PNG** (not JPEG) so the transparency is preserved.
